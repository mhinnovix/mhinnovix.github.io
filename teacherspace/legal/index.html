2.2 Google Drive backup

  • When you allow it, the app backs up your Teacher Space data to your own Google Drive, in an app-specific storage area.
  • The app requests only the drive.appdata permission. It can read and write only the backup files it creates — it cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Backup files are encrypted before upload. The last few backups are kept so you can recover from mistakes.
  • MH Innovix does not have access to your Google Drive or your backups.

2.3 Google user data — Limited Use

Teacher Space's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide sign-in and backup features visible to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to others except as needed to provide these features or as required by law, and not read by humans except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or as required by law.

2.4 Files you export

PDFs you create (for example Leave Account, Salary Projection and Mid-Day Meal reports) are saved in your phone's Download/Teacher Space folder. They stay on your phone and are shared only if you choose to share them.

2.5 Permissions on your phone

  • Internet — to sign in, back up, and download notices and holiday lists.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — to save exported PDFs in the Download folder.
  • Folder access (optional) — only if you choose to show files saved before reinstalling the app.

2.6 Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase Authentication and Google Drive), or when required by law.

2.7 Data retention and deletion

  • On your phone: signing out of the app removes your Teacher Space data from that phone. Uninstalling also removes it.
  • Backups: you can delete them in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Teacher Space → Delete hidden app data), or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Sign-in account: to delete your Teacher Space sign-in record, email us from your registered address (see Contact). We will delete it within 30 days.

2.8 Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and backups are encrypted. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

2.9 Children

Teacher Space is meant for teachers and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
