Section 01

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how MH Innovix (“we”, “us”, or “the Developer”) handles information when you use the HPRC Scanner mobile application, browser extension and associated services (collectively, the “Service”). We design HPRC Scanner with an offline-first, privacy-preserving architecture.

1.1 Offline-First Data Storage & PC Live Entry

HPRC Scanner is fundamentally an offline-first utility. For standard usage, extracted student data, marks, and locally generated outputs are stored exclusively on your own device in a secured local database. Please note that the app does not process attendance data, and the physical photographs of register pages are transient—they are processed solely for extraction and are not saved in the app's local database after the scanning is complete. We generally do not upload, mirror, replicate or back up student records to any servers operated by MH Innovix.

Exception for PC Live Entry: If you use the "PC Live Entry" feature to transfer data to your PC/browser extension, the relevant student data is temporarily stored in our secured database solely to facilitate this real-time transfer. This temporary data is automatically purged from our database once the transfer completes.

1.2 AI Processing & Data Logging (Free vs. Premium)

When you initiate a scan, the relevant document image is transmitted securely to the Google Gemini API for optical character recognition and text extraction.

Data Logging & Analysis: To analyze OCR accuracy, troubleshoot issues, and continuously improve the Service, scan inputs (images) and their corresponding text outputs may be temporarily logged in our Google AI Studio console, where they can be reviewed by the developer solely for analytical and developmental purposes.

Tier-Based Privacy: We are implementing a tier-based privacy model for these developer logs. For Free Tier users, this logging remains active to help us train and improve the app's accuracy. For Premium (Subscribed) users, we are transitioning to a strict ephemeral processing pipeline where developer logging is disabled, ensuring that scanned images are not retained or reviewed in our AI console after the extraction completes.

Limited, time-bounded developer logs may be generated strictly for diagnostic and bug-fixing purposes. Such logs are subject to Google Cloud's standard retention and security policies and are never sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used to build a profile of any individual student. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms for upstream handling.

1.3 Cloud Sync & Profile Data

If you create an account, we sync minimal profile data — email address, display name, device identifier, subscription tier and scan-limit balance — to our database. This enables you to seamlessly restore your purchased limits upon re-login. We do not sync passwords, mirror or expose any scanned content, register photographs or student records via the cloud account (with the temporary exception of the PC Live Entry feature described above).

1.4 Analytics, Admin Panel & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics and standard analytics tools to collect anonymised usage data and crash reports. This helps us understand stability issues and aggregate feature usage to improve the Service. These analytics do not include scanned content or personally identifying student information.

1.5 Permissions Requested

  • Camera — to capture register pages.
  • Storage — to save outputs (PDFs, Excel exports) locally.
  • Internet — to call the Gemini OCR API and sync subscription status.
  • Billing — to process In-App Purchases via Google Play Billing.

1.6 Customer Support

When you use the in-app support or contact feature, your name, email address, and message are transmitted directly from the client app to our database, allowing our team to process and respond to your requests via our admin app. As clearly stated on the app screen before submission, your details are shared strictly for the purpose of providing customer support and resolving your queries.

1.7 No Government Affiliation. HPRC Scanner is an independent, third-party productivity utility developed by MH Innovix. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, partnered with, or in any way connected to the West Bengal Board of Primary Education (WBBPE), the SMS Portal, or any state or central government body, department or official portal. References to such portals exist solely to describe interoperability.

1.8 Children's Data

HPRC Scanner is intended for use by educators, school administrators and authorised adults. Although the data being digitised may pertain to minors enrolled in schools, that data never leaves the educator's own device under our architecture. Educators are themselves the data fiduciaries of the records they scan, and must comply with applicable laws including the DPDP Act, 2023.

1.9 Your Choices

You may stop using the Service at any time by uninstalling the app. Account deletion requests (which purge profile and subscription metadata) can be raised via the contact channel in Section 04.
