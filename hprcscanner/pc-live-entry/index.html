Tutorial

PC Live Entry Process

Follow these 9 simple steps to scan your marks via the app and seamlessly auto-fill the SMS portal on your desktop browser.

01

Initial Setup & Profile

Right after your first install, head over to the profile page. Set up your basic details like your default class, subjects, and choose your required scanning page format.

02

Scan Your Register

Come to the app's dashboard and click on "Scan Full Page". Capture a clear, straight picture of your handwritten HPRC register to begin the AI scan.

03

Review & Save Data

Once the scan is complete, a preview table will appear on your screen. Quickly cross-check the scanned data with your physical HPRC register to ensure accuracy, then hit save.

04

Manage Student Profiles

Repeat this process for all your students. You can view all saved records by clicking on "Student Profiles" from the dashboard. If needed, you can easily edit any student's data right from there.

05

Initiate PC Connection

On the Student Profiles page, tap the Three-Dot Menu located at the top right corner and select "Connect to PC".

06

Download Browser Extension

A pop-up will appear. Click "Download Extension" to get the file, transfer it to your laptop, and install it on your browser.

Alternatively, if you are already on your PC, you can click here to download the extension directly.

07

Generate Secure Code

Once you have the extension, tap "Proceed" on the app's pop-up. A unique, secure connection code will be generated and displayed on your mobile screen.

08

Pair Your PC & App

Switch to your PC and open the browser extension. Enter your registered email address along with the code shown in your app, and click Connect. Once connected successfully, you can close the app on your phone.

09

Auto Fill Marks on PC

Open the Banglar Shiksha SMS portal on your PC browser and navigate to the marks entry table. Open the extension, select the exact class, section, evaluation, and subject, then click "Auto Fill Marks". The table will be populated instantly.

Note: For security purposes, once your work is completely finished, please open the extension and click "Log out & Clear Data".
