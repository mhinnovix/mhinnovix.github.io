Section 1

Privacy Policy

At MH Innovix, we believe that your financial applications and demat portfolio data belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy describes how the IPO Tracker Android application collects, protects, and handles your information.

1.1 Local On-Device Database Architecture

IPO Tracker functions as an offline-first local productivity application. All application entries, applicant profiles, PAN cards, Demat BOIDs, broker names, bid lots, and UPI IDs are stored strictly on your device using a local Room SQLite encrypted database. We do not maintain any private web servers that collect, store, or sell your personal financial records.

1.2 Information You Provide

  • Applicant Profile Data: Names, PAN numbers, Demat DP / BOIDs, and UPI IDs saved in your local Applicants Vault for quick bidding and allotment querying.
  • IPO Application Records: Lot quantities, bid prices, application numbers, applied categories (Retail / HNI / Employee / Shareholder), and payment statuses.
  • Imported Files: Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV spreadsheets that you voluntarily select through the Android document picker to populate your records.

1.3 Network Communications & Registrars

When you check allotment results or refresh live market GMP data, network requests are initiated directly from your mobile device to official registrar servers (such as Link Intime, Bigshare Services, KFin Technologies, and Maashitla) and publicly available stock indexing feeds. No intermediate proxy controlled by MH Innovix intercepts or records your PAN numbers during registrar queries.

1.4 Privacy Masking Mode

The application provides an instant Privacy Masking toggle on the Allotment Checker and Application screens. When enabled, applicant names and PAN numbers are automatically masked (e.g., •••••••379H) to prevent shoulder surfing in public places or accidental exposure during screen sharing.

Section 2

Google Drive Backup & Cloud Permissions

IPO Tracker allows users to optionally connect their personal Google account for seamless cloud backup and multi-device restoration.

Strictly Limited Permission Scope (`drive.file`)

The app only requests access to files that it creates itself within your Google Drive account. It cannot read, access, modify, or delete any of your personal photos, documents, spreadsheets, or other files stored in your Google Drive.

When you tap "Backup Now", an encrypted JSON snapshot containing your saved IPO applications and vault accounts is written directly into your private Google Drive app folder. When you restore, the app reads back only this file. You can disconnect your Google account or delete backup snapshots at any time via the in-app Settings → Manage Backups screen or directly from your Google Drive console.
