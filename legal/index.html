Privacy Policy Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which MH Innovix ("we", "our", or "us") handles information across our mobile applications, utilities, and web services. We build our apps on a Local-First Privacy Architecture, meaning your personal and operational data stays under your control on your own device unless you explicitly initiate a cloud backup or sync feature.

1. Local Data Storage

Most utility and offline applications developed under MH Innovix store user-generated records, configurations, and databases locally on your device (using Android Room / SQLite storage). We do not transmit, read, or harvest your local database records onto external servers.

2. Google Account & Google Drive Integration (OAuth 2.0)

Certain MH Innovix applications offer an optional Backup & Restore feature powered by Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. When you choose to connect your Google Account:

  • Basic Account Identifier: We use your email address and profile identifier strictly inside the app interface so you can see which Google account is currently linked for backup.
  • Restricted Drive Scopes: Our applications only request restricted, non-invasive Google Drive scopes (such as drive.file or drive.appdata). This means our apps can only access specific backup files or hidden app configuration folders created by the app itself. We cannot view, modify, or access any of your personal photos, documents, or other existing files in your Google Drive.
  • User-Initiated Transfer: Backup archives are uploaded directly from your device to your personal Google Drive storage and restored back to your device only when triggered by you.
Google API Services User Data Policy Disclosure:
MH Innovix's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never use Google user data for advertising, nor do we sell or share it with third-party data brokers.

3. Diagnostics, Crash Reporting & Analytics

To ensure app stability and deliver timely updates, our applications may utilize Google Firebase services (such as Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google Analytics) and Google Play Services. These tools collect anonymized, non-personally identifiable technical diagnostics (such as device model, OS version, and crash stack traces) solely to fix bugs and improve app performance.

4. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can clear all local data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or uninstalling the application.
  • Google Drive Backups: You remain the sole owner of backup files stored in your Google Drive. You can delete them directly from your Google Drive account or disconnect the app from your Google Account Permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
